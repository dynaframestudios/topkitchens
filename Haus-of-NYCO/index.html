About Häus Of NYCO

At Häus of NYCO, we believe sound is an essential part of everyday life. It connects us to the music we love, the people we speak to and the moments we enjoy. Our approach is to bring together a curated collection of audio products with an emphasis on quality, innovation and practical design.

Our collection spans wireless headphones, wired computer headsets, open-ear audio and USB audio solutions. We recognise that every listener has different needs: some value the freedom of wireless connectivity, while others prefer the simplicity of a wired connection or the open-ear listening experience.

By offering a variety of audio styles and features, Häus of NYCO aims to make it easier to find a product that complements your lifestyle. Explore our range, discover the features that matter to you and get in touch with our team if you would like more information before choosing your next audio product.
